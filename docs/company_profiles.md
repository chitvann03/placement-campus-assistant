# Campus Placement Company Profiles

Recruitment profiles of major companies visiting Indian engineering campuses, covering teams, rounds, culture, and placement statistics for RAG indexing.

---
## 1. Google India

- **About the Company**: Global technology leader in search, cloud, AI, and Android; India hubs build core platforms for Search, Maps, and GCP.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Gurugram, Mumbai; primary campus hiring targets Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: Google Cloud, Core Search, Google Pay, Android, Ads.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: Software Engineering Intern, Software Engineer (SDE-1 / L3).
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *Online Assessment*: 2 DSA problems on Google platform (90 mins).
  2. *Technical 1*: Trees, graphs, and algorithmic complexity.
  3. *Technical 2*: Dynamic programming and algorithms.
  4. *Googliness & Leadership*: Behavioral fit, ethics, and ambiguity.
- **Work Culture**: High autonomy, flat hierarchy, 20% innovation time, psychological safety.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Ladder to Principal Engineer, cross-team transfers, global relocation.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 6 offers; Base: ₹22 LPA; Total CTC: ₹48-52 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: Top compensation, world-class amenities, peer caliber; occasional launch crunch.

## 2. Microsoft India

- **About the Company**: Enterprise software leader across cloud (Azure), productivity (M365), and developer platforms, with IDC as a major global hub.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Noida, Pune; campus recruitment targets Hyderabad and Bengaluru.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: Azure Cloud, M365, Developer Division, Windows OS, Bing AI.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: Software Engineer (L59/L60), Cloud Support Engineer, Solution Architect.
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *Online Coding*: 3 DSA problems on arrays, strings, trees (75 mins).
  2. *Technical 1*: Core data structures, recursion, linked lists.
  3. *Technical 2*: Algorithms, Low-Level Design (LLD), OOP.
  4. *AA / Manager*: System fundamentals (OS/DBMS) and growth mindset.
- **Work Culture**: Empathetic leadership, growth mindset, great work-life balance, hybrid flexibility.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Transparent promotions, technical mentoring, internal transfers, patent sponsorships.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 12 offers; Base: ₹18 LPA; Total CTC: ₹42-46 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: Supportive culture, high job stability, and mature engineering practices.

## 3. Amazon India

- **About the Company**: E-commerce and cloud (AWS) powerhouse whose India engineering teams build core services for retail and cloud infrastructure.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi NCR; campus hires join Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: AWS, Consumer Retail, Fulfillment Tech, Alexa AI, Seller Services.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: Software Development Engineer I (SDE-1), Quality Assurance Engineer (QAE).
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *Online Assessment*: Code debugging, 2 DSA questions, Leadership Principles (LP).
  2. *Technical 1*: Heaps, tries, algorithms, LP inquiries.
  3. *Technical 2*: Algorithmic optimization, clean OOP, LP scenarios.
  4. *Bar Raiser*: Scalability, problem-solving, deep LP scrutiny.
- **Work Culture**: Customer-obsessed, frugal, high-ownership, fast-paced, metrics-driven.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Rapid technical mastery, ownership of distributed systems, progression to Senior SDE.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 18 offers; Base: ₹16-18 LPA; Total CTC: ₹38-44 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: Outstanding technical growth and scale; demanding on-call rotations and timelines.

## 4. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

- **About the Company**: Premier IT services enterprise under the Tata Group, serving global Fortune 500 enterprises.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Mumbai, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Kochi.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: Digital Enterprise Solutions, BFSI Tech, Cloud Infrastructure, TCS Interactive.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: TCS Ninja (Standard), TCS Digital (Advanced), TCS Prime (R&D / Advanced Engineering).
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *TCS NQT*: Aptitude, numerical ability, verbal reasoning, programming.
  2. *Technical*: Programming in C/C++/Java/Python, DBMS, OOP, academic projects.
  3. *Managerial & HR*: Behavioral fit, communication, relocation readiness, shift flexibility.
- **Work Culture**: Stable, ethical, family-friendly, high job security, corporate hierarchy.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Internal Wings/Elevate exams for accelerated hikes and promotions; overseas projects.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 140 offers; Ninja: ₹3.6 LPA; Digital: ₹7.2 LPA; Prime: ₹9.0 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: Praised for job security and work-life balance; tech stack depends on project allocation.

## 5. Infosys Limited

- **About the Company**: Global IT and consulting leader renowned for digital transformation, cloud solutions, and its Mysore Global Education Center.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Bhubaneswar, Chandigarh, Mysuru; initial training is at Mysuru.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: Infosys Cobalt, Core Banking (Finacle), AI & Automation, Enterprise Applications.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: Systems Engineer (SE), Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE), Specialist Programmer (SP).
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *Online Assessment*: Logical reasoning, mathematical aptitude, algorithmic coding.
  2. *Technical*: OOP concepts, web technologies, basic data structures, project architecture.
  3. *HR*: Communication fluency, culture fit, shift flexibility, credential verification.
- **Work Culture**: Training-centric, structured, supportive for freshers, world-class campus infrastructure.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Bridge to Developer and Bridge to Consulting fast tracks for higher compensation tiers.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 110 offers; Systems Engineer: ₹3.8 LPA; DSE: ₹6.5 LPA; Specialist Programmer: ₹9.5 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: Acclaimed for Mysore training; career advancement requires self-driven upskilling.

## 6. Goldman Sachs India

- **About the Company**: Elite investment banking firm whose India Engineering Division builds quantitative finance and trading systems.
- **India Offices & Locations**: Bengaluru, Hyderabad; primary campus recruitment is centered at Bengaluru.
- **Teams That Hire from Campus**: Global Markets Engineering, Asset & Wealth Management, Transaction Banking, Core Engineering.
- **Typical Roles Offered**: Engineering Analyst (Full-Time), Summer Analyst (Intern).
- **Selection Process**:
  1. *Aptitude & Coding*: Math/probability MCQs, CS fundamentals, 2 DSA coding problems.
  2. *Technical 1*: Trees, hash maps, heaps, dynamic programming.
  3. *Technical 2*: Low-level design, concurrency, multi-threading, database optimization.
  4. *Values & Behavioral*: Commercial awareness, stress tolerance, collaborative mindset, integrity.
- **Work Culture**: High-performance, meritocratic, intellectually stimulating, fast-paced with exposure to global finance.
- **Growth Opportunities**: Advancement from Analyst to Associate in 2-3 years, large bonuses, international mobility.
- **Past Placement Stats (Mock 2024-25)**: 8 offers; Base: ₹20-24 LPA; Total CTC: ₹32-38 LPA.
- **Employee Reviews Summary**: High-caliber peers, steep financial tech learning curve, elite pay; demanding during market volatility.
