# Campus Placement Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Answers to 20 key questions on recruitment rules, eligibility, interview formats, and campus placement guidelines for Indian engineering colleges, structured for candidate preparation and RAG indexing.

---

### 1. What is the step-by-step campus placement process?

The season starts with Pre-Placement Talks (PPT). Eligible candidates register on the college portal, complete online aptitude/coding assessments, and shortlisted students face technical interviews, group discussions, and HR rounds, culminating in final job offers.

---

### 2. What are the general academic eligibility rules to participate in campus placements?

Students must be in their final year, meet minimum CGPA cutoffs (typically 6.0–7.0+), maintain 75% attendance, have zero disciplinary records, and clear all institutional dues before placement registration.

---

### 3. What are the typical CGPA cutoffs for tier-1 tech firms versus mass recruiters?

Tier-1 product firms (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) typically require 7.5 to 8.5+ CGPA with zero backlogs. Mass IT recruiters (TCS, Infosys) require 6.0 to 6.5 CGPA with relaxed rules for cleared backlogs.

---

### 4. How do company tiers like Regular, Dream, and Super Dream work?

Companies are categorized by package: Regular/Core (up to ₹6 LPA), Dream (₹6 LPA to ₹12 LPA), and Super Dream (above ₹12 LPA). Holding a lower-tier offer allows upgrading to higher-tier opportunities.

---

### 5. What is the college policy on holding multiple placement offers?

The college follows a "One-Student-One-Job" rule. After securing an offer in a tier, candidates cannot apply for equal or lower tiers, but may upgrade by interviewing for higher tiers.

---

### 6. How do active and historical backlogs affect placement eligibility?

Most firms require zero active (standing) backlogs during recruitment. IT service firms permit cleared past backlogs, whereas top product firms strictly require zero backlogs throughout the degree.

---

### 7. What is the mandatory dress code for campus placement drives and interviews?

Formal business attire is mandatory. Men wear formal shirts, trousers, neckties, blazers, and polished leather shoes. Women wear formal trousers with shirts, business suits, or formal Indian salwar suits.

---

### 8. What is the difference between an internship, a PPO, and a full-time placement offer?

An internship is practical training (2 to 6 months). A Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) converts high-performing interns into permanent hires before graduation. Full-time offers begin immediately post-graduation.

---

### 9. When is the ideal time to start placement preparation, and what is the roadmap?

Semesters 4–5: master Data Structures & Algorithms and OOP. Semester 6: develop full-stack/AI projects and practice aptitude. Semester 7: polish resumes, practice mock interviews, and attend active placement drives.

---

### 10. What are the key resume formatting tips approved by the placement cell?

Use a single-page reverse-chronological layout with clean headings. Highlight quantifiable achievements (e.g., "reduced latency by 30%"), include verified GitHub/LinkedIn links, proofread for zero errors, and ensure authentic academic scores.

---

### 11. How should students prepare for the online aptitude test round?

Practice quantitative math, logical reasoning, and verbal ability on platforms like IndiaBIX. Focus on speed and accuracy, simulate timed tests, and identify negative marking rules before answering.

---

### 12. What should candidates expect in a technical interview round?

Expect live DSA coding, explaining time/space complexities, and core CS theory (OOP, DBMS, OS, Computer Networks). Interviewers also probe deeply into resume projects and design choices.

---

### 13. How are Group Discussions (GD) conducted and evaluated?

Groups of 8–12 candidates discuss a topic for 15 minutes. Evaluators score clarity, active listening, structured reasoning, and leadership. Initiate constructively or summarize group consensus effectively.

---

### 14. What questions are commonly asked in the HR and behavioral interview round?

HR rounds evaluate culture fit, teamwork, and goals. Common questions: "Tell me about yourself," "Describe a conflict you resolved," "Why our company?", "Strengths and weaknesses?", and relocation readiness.

---

### 15. Can students from non-computer branches (Mechanical, Civil, EE) apply for software roles?

Yes, IT services and select product firms welcome all engineering branches. Non-CS candidates must demonstrate strong coding fundamentals, DSA proficiency, and software projects on their resumes.

---

### 16. What penalty applies if a student registers for a drive but fails to appear?

Absenteeism after registering or getting shortlisted is considered indiscipline. Offending students face a no-show penalty, usually debarment from the next 2 to 3 campus recruitment drives.

---

### 17. What documents must students carry for in-person placement drives?

Carry 3 printed resumes, college ID, government photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN), 4 passport photographs, and original and photocopied mark sheets (10th, 12th, and all college semesters) in a folder.

---

### 18. Are students permitted to negotiate offered CTC or job locations during campus drives?

No. Campus placement compensation and terms are fixed by institutional agreements with recruiters. Attempting individual salary negotiation during interviews is unprofessional and risks offer cancellation.

---

### 19. What should a candidate do if an online test crashes or faces technical issues?

Immediately alert the lab invigilator or placement coordinator with photographic proof of the error screen. The placement cell coordinates with recruiters for re-test slots or token resets.

---

### 20. How can students contact the Training & Placement (T&P) Cell for assistance?

Visit the T&P Office (Admin Block, Room 302) Monday–Saturday (9:00 AM–5:00 PM), email placements@college.edu, or contact branch student coordinators through the central placement portal.
